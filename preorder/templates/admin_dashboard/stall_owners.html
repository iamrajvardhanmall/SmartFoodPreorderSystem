How Stall Owner Accounts Work
  • Each stall owner is assigned to one specific food stall.
  • A unique 5-digit Stall Code is auto-generated as their owner ID.
  • They log in with their username & password to view all orders placed at their stall.
  • They can mark orders as Completed or Cancelled directly.
